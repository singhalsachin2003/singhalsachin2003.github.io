### Question 1

An investment offers a stated annual rate of 8% compounded quarterly. The effective annual rate is closest to:

> r = 8% · m = 4

A. 8.00%  
B. 8.24%  
C. 8.30%  
D. 8.33%  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** EAR = (1 + 0.08/4)⁴ − 1 = 1.02⁴ − 1 = 8.243%. More frequent compounding always raises the effective rate above the stated rate.

*Reading: Quantitative Methods — Types of Financial Returns*

</details>

### Question 2

A researcher rejects the null hypothesis at the 5% significance level when the null is in fact true. This is best described as:

A. A Type I error  
B. A Type II error  
C. A power failure  
D. A specification error  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**A.** Rejecting a true null is a Type I error, and its probability equals the significance level chosen. Failing to reject a false null is Type II.

*Reading: Quantitative Methods — Estimation and Hypothesis Testing*

</details>

### Question 3

A sample of 25 monthly returns has a mean of 1.2% and a standard deviation of 3.0%. The standard error of the sample mean is closest to:

> x̄ = 1.2% · s = 3.0% · n = 25

A. 0.12%  
B. 0.60%  
C. 3.00%  
D. 1.20%  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** The standard error is s/√n = 3.0%/5 = 0.60%. It falls with the square root of sample size, not with sample size itself.

*Reading: Quantitative Methods — Estimation and Hypothesis Testing*

</details>

### Question 4

In a simple linear regression the coefficient of determination is 0.64. The correlation between the dependent and independent variable is:

> R² = 0.64

A. 0.41  
B. 0.64  
C. 0.80 in absolute value  
D. 1.28  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**C.** In simple linear regression R² is the squared correlation, so |r| = √0.64 = 0.80. The sign follows the sign of the slope coefficient.

*Reading: Quantitative Methods — Applications of Simple Linear Regression in Finance*

</details>

### Question 5

Which measure of central tendency is most appropriate for describing a compound annual growth rate over multiple periods?

A. Arithmetic mean  
B. Geometric mean  
C. Harmonic mean  
D. Mode  

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Show the answer</summary>

**B.** The geometric mean accounts for compounding and gives the constant rate that reproduces the ending value. It is always ≤ the arithmetic mean.

*Reading: Quantitative Methods — Statistical Characteristics of Asset Returns*

</details>
